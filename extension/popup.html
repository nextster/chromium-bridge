Local browser access

Chromium Bridge lets local tools you install read and control websites in this browser profile.

  • Page content, URLs, and browser interactions may be sent to the local Native Messaging host.
  • Cookie access is optional. DevTools operations remain blocked until you approve local access.
  • The extension has no developer-operated server. Your local tools decide where their outputs are processed.
